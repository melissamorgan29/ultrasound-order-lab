WHY THIS LEARNING EXPERIENCE?

Why use interactive clinical simulation?

Worksheets can help students recognize an error, but a clinical encounter also requires deciding what to do next, who has authority to correct the record, and how to confirm that the problem is resolved. This simulation provides a safe place to practice those connected decisions before patient care.

Authentic practice

Fictional provider orders, facility requisitions, and workplace conversations reflect the documents and communication students will encounter.

Decisions with consequences

Branching responses make students address unsafe choices and practice recovery instead of simply seeing an answer key.

Feedback and reflection

Immediate preceptor coaching, replayable narration, and before-and-after checks support repeated practice and self-assessment.

Technology note: This is a scripted, browser-based clinical simulation with speech synthesis, not an AI diagnostic system or a live conversational agent. Voice quality depends on available device voices. No real patient information should be entered.
